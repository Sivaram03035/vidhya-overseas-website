Study in Austria – Free &Affordable European Education and Research

Austria provides a strong academic environment, internationally recognised universities and relatively affordable public higher education for international students. Public universities generally charge non-EU students around €726.72 per semester, plus student union and other contributions, subject to institutional and legal exceptions. Popular courses include Business Administration, Finance, Economics, International Management, Mechanical Engineering, Computer Science, Tourism Management, Music, Architecture and Biotechnology. Austria is especially suitable for students looking for affordable European education and opportunities in business, tourism, engineering and research. German-language proficiency can be important for academic and employment opportunities.

Indicative tuition fees

Indicative tuition: Approximately €1,450–€1,600 per year at many public universities, excluding additional charges.

Important: Tuition figures and programme information are indicative and may change by university, course, student status and academic year. Verify current fees, admission requirements, scholarships, visa rules and work permissions with official university and government sources before publishing or making decisions.
