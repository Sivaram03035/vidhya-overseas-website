Study in Denmark – Practical Learning and Global Business Opportunities

Denmark is recognised for its high-quality education, innovative teaching practices and strong connection between universities and industries. It offers programmes suitable for students seeking professional development in business, engineering, technology and sustainability. Popular courses include International Business, Supply Chain Management, Logistics, Finance, Economics, Mechanical Engineering, Renewable Energy, Biotechnology, Computer Science and Design. Education is generally tuition-free for EU/EEA students, while Indian and other non-EU students are required to pay tuition fees. Certain universities provide scholarships and tuition waivers for academically qualified international students.

Indicative tuition fees

Indicative tuition: Approximately €6,000–€18,000 per year for non-EU students.

Important: Tuition figures and programme information are indicative and may change by university, course, student status and academic year. Verify current fees, admission requirements, scholarships, visa rules and work permissions with official university and government sources before publishing or making decisions.
