Study in Estonia – Digital Innovation and Technology-Focused Education

Estonia is recognised for its digital infrastructure, technology ecosystem and innovative education system. It is a suitable destination for students interested in IT, startups, digital business and technology. Popular courses include Computer Science, Software Engineering, Cybersecurity, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Business Analytics, International Business, Finance and Digital Transformation. Some Estonian-taught programmes are tuition-free, while English-taught programmes generally charge fees, although scholarships and tuition waivers may be available. Estonia is especially attractive for students aiming for careers in software, fintech and digital services.

Indicative tuition fees

Indicative tuition: Approximately €3,000–€8,000 per year for many English-taught programmes.

Important: Tuition figures and programme information are indicative and may change by university, course, student status and academic year. Verify current fees, admission requirements, scholarships, visa rules and work permissions with official university and government sources before publishing or making decisions.
