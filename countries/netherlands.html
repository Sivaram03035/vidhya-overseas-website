Study in the Netherlands – International Education and Industry-Focused Programmes

The Netherlands is a leading European destination for international students, offering a wide range of English-taught bachelor's and master's programmes. Its universities are recognised for research, innovation, practical education and international learning environments. Popular programmes include Supply Chain Management, Logistics, International Business, Finance, Economics, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Engineering, Environmental Management and Agricultural Science. The country is particularly attractive for students interested in logistics, international trade and multinational business operations. Non-EU students generally pay higher tuition fees than EU students, and living expenses, particularly accommodation, should be considered when planning the budget.

Indicative tuition fees

Indicative tuition: €8,000–€20,000 per year, with some specialised programmes costing more.

Important: Tuition figures and programme information are indicative and may change by university, course, student status and academic year. Verify current fees, admission requirements, scholarships, visa rules and work permissions with official university and government sources before publishing or making decisions.
