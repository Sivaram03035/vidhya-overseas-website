Study in France – Affordable Education and Global Career Opportunities

France offers internationally recognised education, prestigious universities and specialised business schools. Public universities receive substantial government funding, making higher education comparatively affordable for international students. Indian students can pursue programmes in Business Management, International Business, Finance, Accounting, Luxury Brand Management, Fashion Design, Hospitality, Computer Science, Engineering, Artificial Intelligence and Data Analytics. Public university tuition for non-EU students is generally around €2,900 for bachelor's and €3,900 for master's programmes under the differentiated fee structure, although exemptions and institution-specific charges apply. Private institutions and business schools can charge considerably more. France is an attractive destination for students interested in business, luxury management, fashion and technology.

Indicative tuition fees

Indicative tuition: Around €2,900–€3,900 per year at public universities under the standard differentiated fee structure; private business schools may charge €8,000–€25,000 or more.

Important: Tuition figures and programme information are indicative and may change by university, course, student status and academic year. Verify current fees, admission requirements, scholarships, visa rules and work permissions with official university and government sources before publishing or making decisions.
