Study in Norway – High-Quality Education and Research Opportunities

Norway is recognised for its high academic standards, research facilities and strong focus on sustainability, engineering and environmental studies. Indian students can explore programmes in Renewable Energy, Marine Engineering, Computer Science, Business Administration, Economics, Environmental Science, Petroleum Engineering and Biotechnology. However, the tuition policy has changed: non-EU/EEA students are generally required to pay tuition at public universities, with fees varying by institution and programme. Students should also account for Norway's high living expenses when calculating their overall study budget.

Indicative tuition fees

Indicative tuition: Varies substantially; approximately €10,000–€30,000 or more per year at many institutions.

Important: Tuition figures and programme information are indicative and may change by university, course, student status and academic year. Verify current fees, admission requirements, scholarships, visa rules and work permissions with official university and government sources before publishing or making decisions.
