Study in Luxembourg for free – Finance, Banking and International Business Education

Luxembourg is an attractive European destination for students interested in finance, banking, economics and international business. The country has a strong financial services sector and a multilingual academic environment. The University of Luxembourg offers programmes in Finance, Accounting, Economics, European Governance, Computer Science, Data Science, Law, Business Administration and Engineering. Public university tuition fees are comparatively low for many programmes, although some courses and later study semesters may have different fee structures. Luxembourg can be particularly interesting for students aiming for careers in financial services, investment management, compliance and international organisations.

Indicative tuition fees

Indicative tuition: Many programmes start at around €400 per semester, but fees vary by course and study period.

Important: Tuition figures and programme information are indicative and may change by university, course, student status and academic year. Verify current fees, admission requirements, scholarships, visa rules and work permissions with official university and government sources before publishing or making decisions.
