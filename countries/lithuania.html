Study in Lithuania – Affordable Education and Growing Technology Opportunities

Lithuania offers internationally recognised academic programmes, English-medium courses and comparatively affordable European education. It is becoming increasingly attractive for students interested in fintech, business, information technology and engineering. Popular courses include Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business Administration, International Business, Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Data Analytics, Engineering and Logistics. Universities provide bachelor's and master's programmes with different tuition structures, and scholarship opportunities may be available for eligible applicants.

Indicative tuition fees

Indicative tuition: €3,000–€9,000 per year for many international programmes.

Important: Tuition figures and programme information are indicative and may change by university, course, student status and academic year. Verify current fees, admission requirements, scholarships, visa rules and work permissions with official university and government sources before publishing or making decisions.
